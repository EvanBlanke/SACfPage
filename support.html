Quick Answers

These cover the most common questions, so you might find what you need right here.

Why do I need a Home hub?

Sunrise Alarm creates HomeKit scenes and automations, and Apple only runs automations on a Home hub, which can be a HomePod, Apple TV, or an iPad set up as a hub. Your hub runs your alarms locally, so they start on time even while your iPhone is asleep or away from home.

Without a Home hub, alarms can’t run on their own. This is how Apple Home works for every app, and Apple has step-by-step instructions for setting one up here.

Which devices are supported?

Sunrise Alarm controls the devices you’ve added to the Apple Home app: lights (brightness, color, and color temperature), windows, blinds, and shades (position and tilt), and smart plugs and outlets (on/off).

How do sunset alarms work?

A sunset alarm eases you toward sleep by slowly dimming your lights over however long you like. Just pick Sunset when you create the alarm, choose when it starts and how long it runs, then add your lights. Set any light to 0% and it’ll switch off at the end.

My alarm didn’t run

Start by checking that your Home hub is powered on and connected to your home network. Then open the Apple Home app and make sure the lights in your alarm respond there. If everything looks right and the alarm still doesn’t run, email us and we’ll take a closer look.
